Tamim Al-Kurdi

Age: Young Child

Status: Deprived of Schooling & Child Laborer for Survival

Tamim's Story: Stolen Childhood, Heavy Buckets, and Lost Classrooms

At an age when a little boy’s hands should only be holding colorful crayons, toys, and storybooks, young Tamim’s small, fragile hands are forced to carry heavy, oversized water buckets across dusty, ruined streets. Born into an innocent world that promised warmth, laughter, and early school days, Tamim instead opened his eyes to the terrifying darkness of war, destruction, and utter deprivation.

The cruel famine that swept through Gaza left a deep and painful mark on Tamim’s delicate body. As food supplies completely vanished, this innocent child endured agonizing starvation that caused his body weight to drop drastically to dangerous, unnatural levels. The playful chubby cheeks of childhood faded away, leaving behind hollow eyes and a frail frame—a heartbreaking sight for a young boy who knew nothing of why the world became so cold and empty.

Instead of running through playgrounds or sitting safely in a kindergarten classroom learning his first letters, Tamim was robbed of every single right of childhood. He has experienced no games, no carefree laughter, and no stable days. His precious early education has been entirely put on hold; the time for him to enter school and begin his academic journey is slipping away, and his family is desperately fighting against time to save his mind and future before childhood completely passes him by.

Every day, Tamim walks through the harsh reality of displacement and exhaustion, bearing physical burdens far beyond his tiny shoulders. Carrying heavy containers of drinking water for his family under the scorching sun, his innocence is being consumed by daily labor just to keep his loved ones alive. Yet, beneath the dust and strain, Tamim remains a sweet, pure child who longs for a quiet moment to simply play, hold a notebook, and learn like other children around the world.
