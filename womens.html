Azhar & Asmaa Al-Kurdi

Mother: Azhar (41 Years Old - Household Head)

Daughter: Asmaa (15 Years Old - Only Daughter in the Family & Illness Survivor)

Azhar & Asmaa's Story: An Educated Mother's Grief and a Daughter's Stolen Ambition

Azhar holds a Bachelor’s degree in Accounting—a credential earned through years of hard study and high hopes of building a stable career in Accounting to support her family. Yet, in the total collapse of Gaza's economy under relentless bombardment, her academic degree offers no shelter. Instead of managing financial records in a peaceful office, this educated mother lives in constant terror for the lives of all her children, fighting every single hour to shelter them from bombs, starvation, and complete despair.

As a mother, Azhar’s heart is endlessly torn between securing basic bread for her sons and protecting her 15-year-old daughter, Asmaa—the only girl among her children. Before the war shattered their lives, Asmaa was an outstanding, top-performing student in her school, dreaming of a bright academic future. Today, as the family's cherished only daughter, her heart breaks to see Asmaa completely barred from education, her textbooks and classroom replaced by terrifying nights under airstrikes and grueling daily survival tasks.

Tragically, the catastrophic conditions took a severe physical toll on Asmaa’s young body. Forced to consume contaminated food and unhygienic bread during months of severe famine, Asmaa contracted viral Hepatitis B. Watching her once vibrant, brilliant only daughter suffer from debilitating liver illness—while constantly fearing for the safety and survival of all her children—has been an unbearable torture for Azhar.

Together, Azhar, Asmaa, and the family have endured an exhausting cycle of forced displacement—fleeing repeatedly through destruction and smoke to seek temporary shelter. Every day demands backbreaking physical labor that no woman or young girl should ever have to bear: hauling heavy water containers over miles of rubble, scrubbing laundry by hand using contaminated water, and performing harsh physical chores just to maintain basic sanitation in overcrowded tents.

Despite the overwhelming darkness, Azhar’s fiercest battle remains shielding all her children from danger, securing medical healing for Asmaa’s fragile health, and finding a way to return her high-achieving daughter to a school desk where her bright mind belongs.
