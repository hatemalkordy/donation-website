Mohammed Al-Kurdi

Age: 52 Years Old

Status: Dental Technician (Licensed Medical Practitioner) & Family Pillar

Mohammed's Story: A Father's Dignity, Heavy Water Jugs, and Crushing Sacrifice

For decades, 52-year-old Mohammed stood as the strong, immovable pillar of the Al-Kurdi family. A certified dental technician with a professional practice license from the Ministry of Health, a diploma in dental technology, and years of university education, Mohammed built his entire life around hard work, self-reliance, and quiet dignity. He was a proud father who never imagined a day would come when he could not shield his children from hunger or secure their academic future.

When the catastrophic war engulfed Gaza, Mohammed’s life’s work was extinguished almost overnight. As the economy collapsed into ruin and violent displacement uprooted his home, the crushing financial burden became an inescapable prison. During the darkest weeks of severe famine and skyrocketing inflation, this hardworking professional faced the heartbreaking reality of being completely unable to put a single meal on the table for his family—a humiliation that tore at his deeply held sense of honor and pride.

For a very long time, Mohammed fiercely refused to allow any detail of his suffering or his family’s plight to be published online. His self-respect and dignity made the thought of public appeals unbearable. But as the catastrophe stretched into months, the sheer weight of reality broke his quiet endurance: university tuition fees for his adult children doubled beyond reach, basic flour and food became unaffordable luxuries, and his young family members were forced into grueling physical labor alongside him just to keep starvation from their tent door.

At 52 years of age—a time when a father should be resting and seeing his life's labor bear fruit—Mohammed endures relentless physical strain across every new zone of forced displacement. At every makeshift shelter, he carries heavy, 100-meter loads of drinking water containers on his tired shoulders day after day, walking over jagged rubble under a scorching sun to ensure his children and elderly relatives have water to drink and survive.

Seeing his educated sons and daughters barred from university doors due to unpaid fees, while his family barely scrapes by on a single meal, forced Mohammed to lay down his pride for their sake. He does not seek pity; he seeks the restoration of his children's education and the basic dignity of keeping his family fed and healthy.

His Appeal: Your support for Mohammed directly alleviates a father's agonizing burden. It covers essential food for his family, secures clean water relief, and pays university tuition fees for his children—restoring hope and dignity to a proud man who has sacrificed everything for his family.
