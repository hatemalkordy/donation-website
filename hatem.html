Hatem's Story: Fighting for Education Amidst Ruin

Passionate about web development and artificial intelligence, I am determined to complete my university degree despite current hardships. Ever since I began studying Computer Science, my goal has been to master software engineering and build a career to support my family.

However, the ongoing war in Gaza completely upended our lives. Instead of focusing on my studies and programming projects, my daily focus shifted entirely to securing basic survival needs—a loaf of bread and clean drinking water for my family.

Our struggle worsened through continuous, painful displacement—fleeing from Jabalia to Rafah, then to Al-Zawayda, Gaza City, and back to Al-Zawayda. Throughout this ordeal, we have faced severe food shortages and ongoing hardships that strain us every day.

Despite these conditions, I persevered and completed my first university year. However, university tuition fees have increased significantly beyond what we can afford. In our current situation of displacement and extreme poverty in Gaza, paying these fees is impossible.

Working to earn an income or cover these expenses is also not an option. Living inside Gaza under severe economic collapse, complete lack of job opportunities, continuous electricity and internet blackouts, alongside having my primary tools—my laptop and phone—broken, I am completely unable to work or practice freelance web development.

My Appeal: I am a dedicated student deprived of my basic right to education. Your support is not just about helping me pay university fees or fix my essential tools; it is first and foremost about providing basic food sustenance and rescuing a human life and future from total loss.
