Sobhia Al-Kurdi

Age: Elderly Family Member

Status: Elderly Woman & Chronic Medical Patient

Sobhia's Story: Fragile Health, Endless Exile, and Silent Pain

At an age when a mother should be surrounded by peace, dignity, and gentle comfort, Sobhia finds herself trapped in a relentless storm of war, displacement, and debilitating illness. As an elderly woman bearing the heavy burden of multiple chronic health conditions—including severe hypertension (high blood pressure) and obesity—every single day is a grueling test of human endurance.

Her body, worn thin by time and illness, suffers from severe fluid retention, causing painful swelling in her legs and feet that has led to distressing skin ulcerations and open sores. Every step she takes is an agonizing ordeal; even resting or finding a few hours of sleep offers no solace, as the constant throbbing pain and breathing difficulties rob her of basic comfort. Before the catastrophe struck, her health was fragilely managed through specialized daily medications and strict care—treatments that have now completely vanished amidst total health system destruction.

Sobhia’s journey through this war is a tragic tapestry of unending exile. She has been forced to endure an exhausting, repetitive cycle of displacement—fleeing from Jabalia to Al-Zawayda, back to Jabalia, south to Gaza City, and once more back to Al-Zawayda. For an elderly woman unable to walk easily, being uprooted time after time means being carried through dust and destruction, leaving behind her bed, her medical routine, and the quiet dignity of home for overcrowded makeshift shelters.

Compounding her medical plight is the devastating reality of widespread famine. Sobhia has lived through the darkest months of starvation and continues to endure its harsh lingering effects. Beyond the desperate need for basic bread, her delicate condition desperately requires proper, digestible nutrition and vital medical therapeutics. Her elderly husband (the family Sheikh) also suffers alongside her, enduring the brutal physical strain of displacement while attempting to care for his ailing wife under impossible conditions.

Sobhia does not ask for luxury; she simply longs for relief from constant physical torment—the essential medicine to calm her blood pressure, specialized care for her ulcerated feet, wholesome food her body can digest, and an end to the agonizing struggle of forced relocation.
