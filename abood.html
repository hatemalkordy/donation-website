Abdullah Al-Kurdi

Age: 19 Years Old

Status: High School Graduate & Daily Laborer

Abdullah's Story: Fatherhood and Survival Amidst Loss

At just nineteen years old, Abdullah was supposed to be stepping into the hopeful halls of a university, building a life around books, lectures, and big dreams. He persevered through high school and earned his Tawjihi certificate under the terrifying roar of airstrikes and constant uncertainty. But before he could even celebrate his academic endurance, tragedy redefined his universe forever: his beloved wife was killed in the war, leaving him widowed in the blink of an eye—holding their tiny, motherless baby daughter in his arms.

Overnight, a teenage boy was thrust into the profound and heartbreaking reality of single fatherhood amidst complete devastation. The infant shown cradled gently in his arms became his entire world, his daily motivation, and his most painful vulnerability. While grieving the loss of his partner, Abdullah had no time to mourn in peace; he had to keep their daughter alive in a place where even a drop of clean water or a sip of milk is a luxury won through agonizing effort.

The nightmare deepened through a relentless cycle of displacement. With his infant wrapped in whatever blankets they could salvage, Abdullah and his family fled from Jabalia to Rafah, then to Al-Zawayda, north to Gaza City, and back again to Al-Zawayda. Each forced move meant walking through rubble, sleeping under makeshift nylon tents, and enduring extreme hunger, skyrocketing inflation, and the paralyzing fear that he would not be able to feed his baby girl the next morning.

Refusing to surrender to despair, Abdullah threw himself into backbreaking physical labor to secure a few humble coins for daily bread and milk. He stood for grueling hours in the suffocating heat of community soup kitchens (Tekkeya), prepared food in local falafel shops, operated traditional mud ovens under scorching flames, and sat on dusty pavement alongside his brother Hatem, manually polishing shoes for passersby. Every single drop of sweat was spent with one thought in mind: ensuring his daughter survives another day.

Yet, amidst the total economic collapse of Gaza, even endless days of exhausting manual labor cannot keep pace with the unbearable cost of basic survival, let alone allow him to save a single unit of money toward future university tuition. Abdullah remains fiercely determined to shield his daughter from darkness, honor his late wife's memory, and one day sit in a university classroom—if given the lifeline to do so.

His Appeal: Support for Abdullah provides immediate nutrition and care for his motherless young daughter, covers basic food survival for his family, and offers him a chance to pursue higher education when circumstances allow.
