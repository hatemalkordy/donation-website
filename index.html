Beyond the Struggle: Our Story

Every story in a time of crisis is a battle for survival, but ours is also a battle to protect human dignity, dreams, and the future of an entire family.

We are the Al-Kurdi family, facing severe hardship, continuous displacement, and the total loss of life’s basic necessities.

When we were forced to flee Jabalia, we escaped with nothing more than the clothes on our backs and our personal identity documents. We had to restart our lives from absolute zero under incredibly harsh conditions, and to this day, we are still unable to secure the most basic necessities—from essential clothing to daily survival needs.

For Dr. Mohammed, a dedicated dentist, life was once about healing others and providing a stable home. Today, after multiple forced displacements—from Jabalia to Rafah, then through endless relocations across Al-Zawayda and Gaza—he finds himself stripped of his clinic and unable to bear the overwhelming costs of sustaining his family.

Our reality today is lived under the harsh conditions of a nylon tent. We struggle daily to afford basic living essentials, while our elders, like Sobhia, face chronic health issues such as hypertension without access to steady medication or care.

Mothers like Azhar live in constant fear for their children, Tamem and Asmaa, who are denied the simple joys of childhood, play, and basic schooling.

Young ambition is also fading under this weight. Hatem and Abood, two promising young men eager to complete their university degrees, see their futures slipping away without the means to continue their education.
